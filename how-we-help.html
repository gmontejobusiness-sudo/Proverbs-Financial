How we help

The best strategy is
the one you can live with.

Our job is to bring the people we serve the sharpest strategy available — and to know you well enough to tell you when the sharpest strategy isn't the right one. A plan that keeps you up at night, or is too complex, too time-consuming, or too risky for who you are, isn't a plan worth having. So we weigh every recommendation on more than its return: what it costs you in peace of mind, simplicity, and time with the people you love. Relationships, finances, health, and purpose — we keep all four in balance.

Common questions

Good questions,
honest answers.

How are you paid?
We keep it simple and transparent. Proverbs Wealth is a fee-based fiduciary firm — we're compensated directly by the people we serve, not through commissions for selling products. That keeps our advice aligned with your best interest, and means you'll always know exactly what you're paying and why.
Are you a fiduciary?
Yes — always. As a registered investment adviser, we're legally and ethically bound to act in your best interest at all times. It isn't a marketing line for us; it's the foundation of every recommendation we make.
Who do you typically work with?
Two groups, mostly: high-earning professionals and business owners in their prime earning years who want their growing wealth structured well, and those within about five years of retirement who want confidence their savings will carry them for life. If that sounds like you, we'd love to talk.
Do you offer full financial planning, or just investment management?
Both — and they work together. We build a comprehensive plan around your life (income, taxes, retirement, equity comp, and legacy), then manage your investments in service of that plan — not the other way around.
Do we have to meet in person, or can we work together remotely?
Whatever fits your life. Many of the people we serve work with us primarily by phone and video, with in-person conversations when it helps. Relationship and responsiveness matter far more than a zip code.
What does working together actually look like?
It starts with an unhurried conversation. From there we listen, design a written plan, walk you through exactly how it works, and then stay with you — adjusting through every season. You'll always have a real advisor who knows your name and your goals.
How do I get started?
Schedule a conversation. There's no cost and no obligation — just a chance to talk through where you are and whether we're the right fit. If we are, we'll take it one thoughtful step at a time.
