Chapter Two · The Accumulator

Building wealth with intention

You're an executive or owner still climbing.

RSUs vesting on a schedule. Concentrated company stock and the tax bill that comes with success. A business that holds most of your net worth. You're doing well — you want it structured. We help executives and owners diversify beyond a single stock or company and turn a high income into lasting, independent wealth that buys back your time.

  • RSUs, equity comp & concentrated-stock strategy
  • Building wealth outside a closely-held business
  • Tax planning, diversification & investment structure
  • Cash flow, saving & the road to independence
