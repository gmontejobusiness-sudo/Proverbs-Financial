Chapter One · Building Wealth

Building wealth with intention

You're an executive or owner still climbing.

RSUs vesting on a schedule. Concentrated company stock and the tax bill that comes with success. A business that holds most of your net worth. You're doing well — you want it structured. We help executives and owners diversify beyond a single stock or company and turn a high income into lasting, independent wealth — so you build for tomorrow while still enjoying your life today.

  • RSUs, equity comp & concentrated-stock strategy
  • Building wealth outside a closely-held business
  • Tax planning, diversification & investment structure
  • Living well now — not just saving for later
Chapter Two · The Five-Year Horizon

Living the wealth you built

You're within five years of retirement.

You've spent decades building. Now the question quietly changes from "how do I save?" to "will I be okay — and will this be a life I actually love?" From here it's about strategy: turning what you've built into dependable income, knowing which accounts to draw from first, and keeping taxes low the whole way — so you never have to second-guess your paycheck in retirement.

  • Retirement income — a dependable paycheck for life
  • Which accounts to draw from, and when
  • Tax-strategic withdrawals & Roth conversions
  • Social Security & pension timing
  • Legacy, longevity & healthcare planning

How we work

The best strategy is
the one you can live with.

Our job is to bring you the sharpest strategy available — and to know you well enough to tell you when the sharpest strategy isn't the right one. A plan that keeps you up at night, or is too complex, too time-consuming, or too risky for who you are, isn't a plan worth having. So we weigh every recommendation on more than its return: what it costs you in peace of mind, simplicity, and time with the people you love. Relationships, finances, health, and purpose — we keep all four in balance.
