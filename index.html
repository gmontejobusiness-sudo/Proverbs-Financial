Chapter One · Building Wealth

Building wealth with intention

You're a professional or business owner in your prime earning years.

RSUs vesting on a schedule. Concentrated company stock and the tax bill that comes with success. A business that holds most of your net worth — anything from a fifteen-person company to a team of one. You're doing well; you want it structured. We help high-earning professionals and business owners align their cash flow to their goals, diversify beyond a single stock or company, and turn a strong income into lasting, independent wealth — so you build for tomorrow while still enjoying your life today.

  • Aligning cash flow to your financial goals
  • RSUs, equity comp & concentrated-stock strategy
  • Building wealth outside a closely-held business
  • Tax planning, diversification & investment structure
  • Living well now — not just saving for later

Chapter Two · The Five-Year Horizon

Living the wealth you built

You're within five years of retirement.

You've spent decades building. Now the question quietly changes from "how much do I need to save?" to "how do I live off everything I've saved, for the rest of my life?" Retirement investing is different from accumulation — instead of simply growing a portfolio, we design strategies built around dependable, tax-efficient income. We help you know which accounts to draw from, and when, so you never have to second-guess your paycheck in retirement.

  • Retirement income portfolio design & strategic asset allocation
  • Retirement income — a dependable paycheck for life
  • Which accounts to draw from, and when
  • Tax-strategic withdrawals & Roth conversions
  • Social Security & pension timing
  • Legacy, longevity & healthcare planning

"For the first time, I know exactly how our savings become a paycheck. That peace of mind is worth everything."

— Recently retired couple, clients since 2016

How we help

Our job is to bring the people we serve the sharpest strategy available — and to know you well enough to tell you when the sharpest strategy isn't the right one. A plan that keeps you up at night, or is too complex, too time-consuming, or too risky for who you are, isn't a plan worth having. So we weigh every recommendation on more than its return: what it costs you in peace of mind, simplicity, and time with the people you love. Relationships, finances, health, and purpose — we keep all four in balance.
